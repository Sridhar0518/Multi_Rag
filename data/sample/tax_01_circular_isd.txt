CBIC Circular No. 999/01/2025-GST (synthetic)
Section 1. Input Service Distributor (ISD). Under Rule 39 of the CGST Rules (synthetic reading), an Input Service Distributor must distribute credit of input services to recipient units using the turnover-based ratio in the preceding financial year.
Section 2. Mandatory registration. From 1 April 2025, every ISD must hold a separate ISD registration; this amendment is prospective and applies only to credits distributed on or after that date.
Section 3. Penalty. Incorrect distribution of credit attracts interest at 18% per annum under Section 50 and a penalty equal to the credit wrongly distributed.
