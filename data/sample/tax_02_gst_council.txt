GST Council Ruling Note R-57 (synthetic)
Section 1. E-invoicing. The GST Council recommended that taxpayers with aggregate turnover above INR 5 crore must issue e-invoices through the Invoice Registration Portal.
Section 2. Clarification (retrospective). The Council clarified that e-invoicing obligations apply to ISD invoices raised on or after 1 October 2024; this is a retrospective clarification of Circular 999/01/2025-GST.
Section 3. CBIC shall issue implementing circulars within 30 days of the Council recommendation.
